### Question

What are the three holding entries, and how do you pick the right one?

### Answer

Draw an extended line from your current position through the holding fix to determine the right holding entry:

- **Direct** - When the extended line essentially takes you directly into the hold. Cross the fix and turn to follow the holding pattern.
- **Parallel** - When the extended line takes you outside the holding pattern, and you are not in a position to directly enter the hold. Cross the fix, turn to parallel the holding course outbound on the **non-holding** side for one minute (for timed legs), then turn 210° towards the protected area to intercept the inbound course.
- **Teardrop** - When the extended line takes you inside the holding pattern, and you are not in a position to directly enter the hold. Cross the fix, turn outbound to a heading 30° offset into the pattern, fly it for one minute (for timed legs), then turn in the direction of the hold to intercept the inbound course.

Protected airspace is designed around pilots flying these three entries at or below the maximum holding speed. Another entry isn't illegal, but combining an unusual entry with excess airspeed can put you outside protected airspace.
