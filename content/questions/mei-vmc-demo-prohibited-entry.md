### Question

What V<sub>MC</sub> demonstration entry is prohibited, and why?

### Answer

**Do not pitch up to a high attitude with both engines operating and then reduce power on the critical engine.** The ACS says the technique is hazardous and may result in loss of airplane control, and the handbook gives the same warning.

- It stacks the two ingredients of a spin at once: a high angle of attack and a sudden asymmetric yawing moment.
- It also arrives at the condition faster than the student, or you, can evaluate it.

The sanctioned entry runs the other way round: establish the single-engine condition first, then decelerate at **1 knot per second, no faster**. That rate is part of the standard, and rushing it is what produces the steep pitch attitude the prohibition is aimed at.
