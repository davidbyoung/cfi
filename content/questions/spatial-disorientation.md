### Question

What is spatial disorientation, what causes it, and how can you avoid it?

### Answer

Spatial disorientation is an incorrect perception of the aircraft’s attitude, position, or motion relative to the Earth. It occurs when visual, vestibular (inner ear), and proprioceptive ("seat-of-the-pants") senses provide misleading or conflicting information, especially when reliable outside visual references are absent. Prevent/correct it by trusting the flight instruments rather than bodily sensations when visual references are unreliable. Maintain instrument proficiency, anticipate conditions that can cause illusions, and avoid unnecessary abrupt head movements during prolonged turns
