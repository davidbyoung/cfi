### Question

What are the types of special use airspace?

### Answer

- **Prohibited area** - Flight is prohibited, period. Established for security or national welfare reasons, e.g., P-56 over the White House.
- **Restricted area** - Hazardous activity such as artillery fire, gunnery, or missile testing. You need permission from the controlling agency unless it's inactive and released.
- **Warning area** - The same kind of activity as a restricted area, but starting 3 NM off the coast over domestic or international waters. It's advisory in nature, because the airspace can't be prohibited.
- **Military Operations Area (MOA)** - Separates military training from IFR traffic. VFR traffic isn't prohibited, but exercise real caution.
- **Alert area** - High volume of pilot training or unusual aerial activity. Everybody in there is equally responsible for seeing and avoiding.
- **Controlled firing area** - Activity stops when an aircraft approaches, which is why these aren't charted.
- **National security area** - Flight avoidance is requested, and can be made mandatory by NOTAM.
