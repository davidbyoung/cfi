### Question

How do you fly a single-engine approach and landing?

### Answer

- Fly a **wider, flatter, longer** pattern than normal, and stay at **Vyse** until landing is assured.
- **Delay the gear and flaps.** Extend the gear only when the landing is certain, and use minimal flaps - many AFMs recommend a partial-flap or no-flap single-engine landing.
- Keep the airplane in **zero sideslip** until you are transitioning to land.
- Plan for a **slightly higher approach speed** and a longer landing roll.
- **Treat it as committed.** Assume no go-around is available.
- Make power changes on the operating engine **smoothly** - a large sudden application at low speed is a Vmc problem.

Late in the flare, as power comes off, the asymmetric yaw goes away, so be ready to release the rudder pressure you have been holding or the airplane will yaw the other way.
