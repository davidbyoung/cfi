### Question

What are the red line and the blue line on a multiengine airspeed indicator?

### Answer

- **Red radial line - Vmc.** The minimum control speed with the critical engine inoperative, as determined during certification.
- **Blue radial line - Vyse.** The best rate of climb speed with one engine inoperative.

Both are single fixed marks, but the speeds they represent move with configuration, weight, CG, and altitude. Treat them as reference points, not guarantees.

The rest of the markings match any airplane: white arc for the flap operating range, green arc for normal operating range, yellow arc for caution, and a red line at Vne.
