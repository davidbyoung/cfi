### Question

How does fuel management change with an engine inoperative?

### Answer

- The operating engine keeps burning from its own side, so over a long single-engine leg the airplane becomes **laterally unbalanced**, which adds to the rolling moment you are already holding.
- **Crossfeed** exists to let the operating engine draw from the tank on the failed side, both to use that fuel and to keep the airplane in lateral balance.
- Crossfeed is normally used in **level cruise**, not during takeoff or landing, and most AFMs prohibit it for takeoff and landing.
- Procedures are airplane-specific and getting them wrong can starve the running engine, so this is a checklist item, not a memory item.

Plan for it: on a long diversion, you want the fuel you can actually reach, and the tank on the dead side is not automatically available.
