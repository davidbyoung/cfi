### Question

When do you start timing in a hold, and how long should your legs be?

### Answer

- Start timing the **outbound** leg when abeam the fix. If you can't determine the abeam position, start timing when the turn outbound is complete.
- Target inbound leg time:
  - At or below 14,000 ft MSL - 1 minute
  - Above 14,000 ft MSL - 1.5 minutes
- Fly the first outbound leg for that same time, then adjust later outbound legs for wind so that the inbound leg comes out at the target time.
  - For example, if your inbound leg was only 50 seconds, then you must extend your outbound leg by another 10 seconds to bring your inbound leg to 1 minute.
- DME and GPS holds are flown to a distance instead of a time. Fly the outbound leg to the charted along-track distance from the fix.
