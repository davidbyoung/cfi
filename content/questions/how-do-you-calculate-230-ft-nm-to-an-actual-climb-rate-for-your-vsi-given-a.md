### Question

How do you calculate 230 ft/NM to an actual climb rate for your VSI given a groundspeed of 90 kt?

### Answer

- Climb rate (fpm) = Gradient (ft/NM) x (Groundspeed (kt) / 60)
- 345 fpm = 230 x 90 / 60
