### Question

Do you need complex or high-performance endorsements to fly a typical light twin?

### Answer

These are separate from the class rating, and you may need both:

- **Complex** ([§ 61.31(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-61/subpart-A/section-61.31#p-61.31%28e%29)) - retractable gear, flaps, and a controllable-pitch propeller. Nearly every light twin qualifies.
- **High performance** ([§ 61.31(f)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-61/subpart-A/section-61.31#p-61.31%28f%29)) - an engine of **more than 200 horsepower**. Note this is measured per engine, not by total installed horsepower.

A common trainer such as a Seminole or Duchess is complex but **not** high performance, because each engine is 180 hp. A Baron or a 310 is both.

Both are one-time endorsements, not recurring currency.
