### Question

Why do you verify before feathering, and how?

### Answer

Because feathering the **operating** engine is unrecoverable at low altitude, and misidentification under stress is common.

To verify: slowly retard the throttle of the engine you believe has failed.

- If **nothing changes** - no change in yaw, sound, or performance - your identification was correct.
- If the airplane's behavior **worsens**, you had the wrong engine. Restore that throttle immediately.

Do it **slowly and deliberately**. The whole point is a reversible test before an irreversible action.

The step is skipped only where the AFM says so, and never as a time-saver.
