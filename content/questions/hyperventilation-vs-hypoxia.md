### Question

How do you tell hyperventilation from hypoxia, and what do you do about it?

### Answer

- Hyperventilation is blowing off too much carbon dioxide by breathing too fast, usually from stress, anxiety, or task saturation.
- The symptoms overlap badly with hypoxia: lightheadedness, tingling, visual disturbance, and eventually unconsciousness.
- Because you can't reliably tell them apart in the moment, if you're at altitude assume hypoxia first. Go to oxygen and descend.
- If that doesn't resolve it, treat the hyperventilation: consciously slow your breathing rate, talk out loud, or breathe into a bag to bring carbon dioxide levels back up.
