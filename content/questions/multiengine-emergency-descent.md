### Question

How do you fly an emergency descent in a twin?

### Answer

- Situations: **depressurization**, **smoke or fire**, or any case where getting down fast is the priority.
- General technique per the AFM: **reduce power**, **extend the landing gear** (and speedbrakes if fitted), and establish a **bank of roughly 30-45°** to keep the nose from rising while accelerating to the target speed.
- Fly at the AFM's recommended airspeed, often near **Vle** or **Vno** depending on the airplane and turbulence.
- Do not exceed **Vle** with the gear down, and respect **Va** in turbulence.
- Level off at a safe altitude - the minimum safe altitude for terrain, or a cabin-altitude-safe level after a depressurization.

The bank is the part people forget: it lets you descend steeply without an excessive nose-low attitude or a large airspeed overshoot.
