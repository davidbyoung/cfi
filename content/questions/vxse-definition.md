### Question

What is V<sub>XSE</sub>, and when would you use it instead of V<sub>YSE</sub>?

### Answer

- **V<sub>XSE</sub>** is the best **angle** of climb speed with one engine inoperative - the most altitude gained per unit of horizontal distance.
- It's slower than V<sub>YSE</sub> and is not marked on the airspeed indicator.
- Use it only when you need to clear an obstacle. The ACS wording for an engine failure with obstructions present is to establish V<sub>XSE</sub>, or **V<sub>MC</sub> + 5 kt**, whichever is higher.
- Once the obstacle is no longer a factor, accelerate to V<sub>YSE</sub>. V<sub>XSE</sub> buys angle at the cost of time spent low and slow, closer to V<sub>MC</sub>.
