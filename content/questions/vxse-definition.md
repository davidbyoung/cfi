### Question

What is Vxse, and when would you use it instead of Vyse?

### Answer

- **Vxse** is the best **angle** of climb speed with one engine inoperative - the most altitude gained per unit of horizontal distance.
- It's slower than Vyse and is not marked on the airspeed indicator.
- Use it only when you need to clear an obstacle. The ACS wording for an engine failure with obstructions present is to establish Vxse, or **Vmc + 5 kt**, whichever is higher.
- Once the obstacle is no longer a factor, accelerate to Vyse. Vxse buys angle at the cost of time spent low and slow, closer to Vmc.
