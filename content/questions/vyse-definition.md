### Question

What is V<sub>YSE</sub>, and why does it get its own blue line?

### Answer

- **V<sub>YSE</sub>** is the best **rate** of climb speed with one engine inoperative - the speed that gives the most altitude gain per unit of time. Above the single-engine **absolute** ceiling, where climb is no longer possible at all, the AFH says V<sub>YSE</sub> also yields the **minimum rate of sink**.
- It is marked with a **blue radial line**, because it's the single most important speed to be at after an engine failure.
- It delivers the best climb only with the airplane cleaned up and in zero sideslip. V<sub>YSE</sub> with the gear down and a windmilling propeller still won't climb.
- Published V<sub>YSE</sub> is for maximum gross weight; the actual speed decreases slightly as weight decreases.
