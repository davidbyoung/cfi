### Question

Decode this NOTAM: !ORD 06/001 ORD RWY 04L/22R CLSD 2106231700-2106232300

### Answer

(! indicates the beginning of a new NOTAM) At O’Hare, this is the 1st NOTAM issued in the month of June, the O’Hare runways 04L/22R are closed from 2021-06-23 at 1700Z to 2021-06-23 at 2300Z
