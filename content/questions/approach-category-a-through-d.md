### Question

What are the A, B, C, and D categories?

### Answer

- Used to categorize aircraft by 1.3 x VSO (IAS) to determine required maneuvering airspace and approach minimums
  - A: < 91 kt
  - B: 91 to < 121 kt
  - C: 121 to < 141 kt
  - D: 141 to < 166 kt
