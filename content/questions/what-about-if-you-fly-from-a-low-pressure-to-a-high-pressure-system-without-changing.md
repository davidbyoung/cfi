### Question

What about if you fly from a low pressure to a high pressure system without changing your altimeter? What happens to your true altitude?

### Answer

True altitude increases. As outside pressure increases, the altimeter interprets the higher pressure as being lower, so you climb to maintain the same indicated altitude.
