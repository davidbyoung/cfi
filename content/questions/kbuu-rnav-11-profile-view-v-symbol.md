### Question

On the KBUU RNAV 11, what is the v symbol in the profile view, and why should you care about it?

### Answer

Visual Descent Point (VDP) - Helpful to know where a normal 3° glidepath would start down to the runway. Going much beyond this point will likely not allow for a stable approach to the runway - a requirement for landing out of an approach.
