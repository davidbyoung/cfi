### Question

How far out is a localizer accurate?

### Answer

- 10 NM within 35° of centerline
- 18 NM within 10° of centerline

### Sources

- [FAA AIM ¶ 1-1 - Navigation aids](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap1_section_1.html)
