### Question

Let’s say you switched airplanes and have a WAAS-equipped GPS for this flight. There are no nonstandard alternate minimums for your selected alternate. What types of approaches can you fly at the alternate, and must the forecast weather be?

### Answer

- WAAS-equipped GPS permits RNAV approaches in addition to all others
- Although LPV provides precision approach-like capability, it is treated as non-precision for this simplified alternate-planning answer. Use 800 ft and 2 SM visibility.
