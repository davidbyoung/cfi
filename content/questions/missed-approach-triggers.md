### Question

When must you go missed?

### Answer

- You reach the DA or MAP without the flight visibility and visual references required by § 91.175(c) to continue.
- You lose the required visual references after descending below DA or MDA, other than during a normal descent to land once you're in a position to make a normal landing.
- You're circling and lose sight of the airport. Turn toward the runway and climb into the published missed approach.
- ATC instructs you to.
- Anything makes a safe approach unlikely: you're unstable or high, you have a navigation or instrument failure, or there's a traffic or wake conflict.

Also worth remembering: obstacle protection assumes you start the missed approach at the DA or MAP, and no lower than the MDA, and that you climb at least 200 ft/NM unless the plate specifies a higher gradient.
