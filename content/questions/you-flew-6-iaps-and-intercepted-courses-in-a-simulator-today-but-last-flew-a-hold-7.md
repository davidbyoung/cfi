### Question

You flew 6 IAPs and intercepted courses in a simulator today, but last flew a hold 7 months ago. What do you need to do to be current?

### Answer

Complete one holding procedure/task, either in an approved simulator or in an airplane with a safety pilot, CFI, or DPE
