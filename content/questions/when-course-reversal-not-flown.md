### Question

When do you skip a published course reversal?

### Answer

A charted procedure turn or hold in lieu of a procedure turn is required when depicted, unless one of these applies:

- **"NoPT"** is charted on the initial segment you're using, including a straight-in TAA sector or a NoPT feeder route or arc
- ATC clears you for a **straight-in** approach
- You're being **radar vectored** to the final approach course
- You're flying a **timed approach** from a holding fix
- You're arriving on a **DME arc** that terminates on the final approach course

Two other things to keep in mind:

- You can still fly the course reversal when it isn't required, but you need an amended clearance from ATC first.
- If the clearance leaves you unsure whether ATC expects a course reversal or a straight-in, ask. [§ 91.123](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-91/subpart-B/subject-group-ECFRe4c59b5f5506932/section-91.123) doesn't leave room for guessing.
