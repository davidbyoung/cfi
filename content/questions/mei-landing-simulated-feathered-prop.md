### Question

What single-engine landing does the ACS specifically require an MEI applicant to perform?

### Answer

At least one landing that you **simultaneously demonstrate and explain** with a **simulated feathered propeller**, the engine set to **zero thrust**, using the manufacturer's recommended procedures.

- It is a simulated feather at zero thrust, not an actual shutdown, which is what makes it repeatable and recoverable.
- It applies to tests in propeller-driven airplanes that do not require a type rating.
- The explaining is not optional. This is the same standard as the rest of the multiengine Tasks.

Plan the approach so that a go-around remains available and so the field is made with the power you actually have. A single-engine approach flown too low or too slow is a worse problem than a long landing, and the evaluator is watching your energy management as much as your narration.
