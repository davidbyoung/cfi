### Question

What happens if you use bank alone, without rudder, after an engine failure?

### Answer

- Holding heading on ailerons alone takes **8-10° of bank** toward the operating engine (AFH Fig 13-16).
- With no rudder input, the ball is displaced **well** toward the operating engine - far more than the third to half a ball of zero sideslip.
- The airplane is left in a **large sideslip toward the operating engine**, and climb performance is greatly reduced.
- The risk of losing directional control is high enough that instructors should not normally demonstrate it.

Wings level and excessive bank bracket zero sideslip from either side: one slips toward the **dead** engine, the other toward the **good** one. Wings level is the moderate error; excessive bank is the severe one.
