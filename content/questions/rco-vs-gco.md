### Question

What's the difference between an RCO and a GCO?

### Answer

- Remote Communications Outlet (RCO) - A direct radio link to Flight Service and can be used by aircraft on the ground or in the air
- Ground Communications Outlet (GCO) - A radio-to-telephone link intended primarily for aircraft on the ground
