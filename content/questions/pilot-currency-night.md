### Question

You only flew at nighttime once in the past 90 days, and it was 10 days ago. You did 4 touch-and-goes. Your friend wants to go up with you for a night flight. Can you take them?

### Answer

No, night currency requires 3 takeoffs and landings to a full stop in the past 90 days.
