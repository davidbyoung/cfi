### Question

How do you unfeather a propeller in flight?

### Answer

- The normal method is to **restart the engine**. Move the propeller control forward, engage the starter, and as the engine turns, the governor restores oil pressure and drives the blades out of feather.
- Many training twins have an **unfeathering accumulator** - a small reservoir holding pressurized engine oil. Moving the propeller control forward releases that stored oil to the hub, kicking the blades out of feather so the engine windmills and starts without prolonged cranking.
- Expect noticeable **vibration and a cold engine**. Warm it before applying significant power.

This is why the ACS requires actual feathering or shutdown only where a **safe landing at an established airport** is possible if the propeller won't unfeather or the engine won't restart.
