### Question

What information do you need to have prior to any flight?

### Answer

- **N**OTAMs
- **W**eather reports and forecasts
- **K**nown ATC delays
- **R**unway lengths
- **A**lternatives available
- **F**uel requirements
- **T**akeoff and landing distances
