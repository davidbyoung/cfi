### Question

Is the actual Vmc on a given flight the same as the red line?

### Answer

No. The red line is **one number** determined under one specific set of certification conditions, and your actual Vmc moves with conditions.

- Most of the time your actual Vmc is **lower** than red line, because you are rarely at the most unfavorable weight, CG, and configuration all at once.
- But it can be **higher** than red line - most notably at an aft CG, or with the failed propeller windmilling when certification assumed a particular position, or if you fail to bank into the operating engine.

Treating red line as a hard floor is the mistake. Being 5 kt above the mark is not the same as having control margin.
