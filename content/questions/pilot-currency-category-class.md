### Question

If you had a multi-engine rating in addition to a single-engine rating and have only done 3 takeoffs and landings during the day in a multi-engine airplane, can you legally take passengers up in a single-engine airplane?

### Answer

No, currency applies to the category _and_ class, meaning you're only current to take passengers up in a multi-engine airplane.
