### Question

Can you descend below the DA without having any of the runway environment in sight?

### Answer

- No. You may not continue below DA/MDA unless the 91.175 requirements are met: required flight visibility, the required runway environment distinctly visible and identifiable, and the aircraft continuously in a position to descend and land using normal maneuvers.
- A momentary dip below DA while arresting the descent and initiating the missed can happen, but that is not permission to continue the approach below DA without the required visual references.
