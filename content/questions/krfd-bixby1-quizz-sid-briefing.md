### Question

Brief the [KRFD BIXBY1.QUIZZ SID](https://www.airnav.com/depart?http://aeronav.faa.gov/d-tpp/2608/00954BIXBY.PDF) for takeoff from runway 7

### Answer

- Verify the chart is correct and up-to-date.
- RNAV 1 (DME/DME/IRU or GPS) and radar are required.
- (Brief the frequencies).
- Top altitude is 3,000 ft.
- Takeoff minimums for runway 7 are 300-⅛ or standard with minimum climb of 230 ft/NM to 1,000 ft, or standard minimums and a standard climb gradient if taking off 1,700 ft prior the departure end of the runway.
- From runway 7, climb on heading 068° or as assigned and expect vectors to BODEE.
- Cross BODEE at 3,000 ft unless given a higher altitude and expect our filed altitude 10 minutes after departure.
- From BODEE, fly 207° to BIXBY.
- From BIXBY, fly the 235° course to QUIZZ.
