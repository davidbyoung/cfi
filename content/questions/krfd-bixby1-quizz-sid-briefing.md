### Question

Brief the KRFD BIXBY1.QUIZZ SID for takeoff from runway 7

### Answer

- Listen for valid chart date, frequencies, required equipment, top altitude, takeoff minimums, crossing restrictions, and routes
- Likely climb at 230 ft/NM to 1,000 ft on a 068° heading, then vectors to BODEE at or above 3,000 ft, then BIXBY, then right turn direct QUIZZ, expecting filed altitude 10 minutes after departure
