### Question

Refer to the surface analysis chart from the source guide.

![Surface analysis chart](../assets/surface-analysis-chart.png)

What is a ridge, and what kind of conditions are associated with it?

### Answer

- An elongated area of relatively high pressure
- It brings clear skies and light winds
