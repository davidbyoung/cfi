### Question

What does the ACS mean when it says you must "demonstrate and simultaneously explain"?

### Answer

Exactly what it says: you fly the maneuver **and** teach it out loud, at the same time, to the standard expected of an instructor.

- Every Task in the ACS's Multiengine Operations area uses that phrasing, and the evaluator must assess **all** of them.
- Flying a maneuver correctly in silence does not satisfy the skill element. Neither does a good briefing followed by a quiet demonstration.
- The explanation has to track what the airplane is doing as it happens, which is why it is harder than either half alone.

This is the single biggest step up from the AMEL practical test. There you had to fly it; here you have to fly it while narrating the control inputs, the reasons for them, and the errors you are watching for, from the right seat.
