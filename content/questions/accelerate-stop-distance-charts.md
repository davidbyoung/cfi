### Question

How do you use the accelerate-stop chart, and what do you do with the answer?

### Answer

- Enter with **pressure altitude, temperature, weight, wind**, and often runway slope and surface condition, and read out the distance to accelerate to the specified speed, lose an engine, react, and stop.
- Compare that to the **runway available**. If accelerate-stop exceeds it, an engine failure near that speed means going off the end.

What to do about it:

- Reduce weight, wait for cooler temperatures, or use a longer runway.
- Adjust the briefed decision point - if accelerate-stop doesn't fit, the honest brief is that above a certain speed you are going flying regardless.

Remember that chart figures assume a **prompt** reaction, a dry runway, and a new airplane. Add margin. And note the chart may not extend to today's conditions, which is itself a result worth respecting.
