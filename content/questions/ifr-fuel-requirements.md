### Question

What are IFR fuel requirements?

### Answer

Enough fuel to fly to the first airport of intended landing, then to the alternate (if one is required), then for 45 minutes at normal cruising speed

- [14 CFR § 91.167 - Fuel requirements for flight in IFR conditions](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-91/subpart-B/subject-group-ECFRef6e8c57f580cfd/section-91.167)
