### Question

How far out does the protected area for an airplane extend during a circling approach?

### Answer

It depends on both the Vso of the airplane and the MDA, but for most general aviation airplanes and approaches, it's 1.3 NM:

| Circling MDA in feet MSL | Category A | Category B | Category C | Category D | Category E |
| ------------------------ | ---------- | ---------- | ---------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| 1,000 or less            | 1.3        | 1.7        | 2.7        | 3.6        | 4.5        |
| 1,001 – 3,000            | 1.3        | 1.8        | 2.8        | 3.7        | 4.6        |
| 3,001 – 5,000            | 1.3        | 1.8        | 2.9        | 3.8        | 4.8        |
| 5,001 – 7,000            | 1.3        | 1.9        | 3.0        | 4.0        | 5.0        |
| 7,001 – 9,000            | 1.4        | 2.0        | 3.2        | 4.2        | 5.3        |
| 9,001 and above          | 1.4        | 2.1        | 3.3        | 4.4        | 5.5        |
