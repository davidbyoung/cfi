### Question

Training twins fly light and often in cool air. How do you show a student realistic single-engine climb performance?

### Answer

**Artificially limit the manifold pressure available on the operating engine.** Most multiengine training happens in four- to six-seat airplanes at weights well below maximum, where single-engine performance at low density altitude is **deceptively good**.

**The cost:** less power on the operating engine also means less asymmetric thrust, so V<sub>MC</sub> falls. You are buying performance realism by spending controllability realism, which is why the handbook's word is **occasionally**.
