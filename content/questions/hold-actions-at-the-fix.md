### Question

What do you do every time you cross the holding fix?

### Answer

The 5 Ts:

- **Turn** - Start the turn to the outbound heading, or the turn called for by your entry.
- **Time** - Start the clock (if using timed legs). Outbound timing begins over or abeam the fix, whichever is later, and inbound timing begins when you're established on the inbound course.
- **Twist** - Set the inbound course on the OBS or HSI so your CDI shows what you expect.
- **Throttle** - Set holding power and airspeed.
- **Talk** - Report entering the hold, and report leaving it.
