### Question

We are planning a flight from KPWK to KSTL. What weather sources are you looking at to determine if we can fly?

### Answer

Start big-picture, then work toward route and terminal details:

- Synopsis (surface analysis, prog charts, cloud charts)
- Adverse conditions (AIRMETs, SIGMETs, and Convective SIGMETs)
- Route and terminal forecasts (METARs and TAFs)
- Winds and Temperatures Aloft (winds, freezing level)
- PIREPs and radar
- NOTAMs
