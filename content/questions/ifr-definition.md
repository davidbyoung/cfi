### Question

What defines IFR conditions? LIFR?

### Answer

IFR conditions are those less than VFR, specifically ceilings 500-1,000 ft and/or visibility 1-3 SM. LIFR, or low instrument flight rules, conditions are technically still IFR, but tell you that ceilings and/or visibility are especially low, specifically ceilings less than 500 ft and/or visibility less than 1 SM. Only highly and recently experienced instrument rated pilots should attempt flying in these conditions.
