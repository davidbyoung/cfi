### Question

What do the landing gear and flaps do to Vmc?

### Answer

- **Extending the landing gear lowers Vmc.** The gear adds keel surface below and ahead of the CG, which acts like a stabilizing fin and resists yaw. Certification is done with the gear **retracted** because that's the more unfavorable case.
- **Flaps generally lower Vmc** slightly. Certification uses the **takeoff** flap position.

Don't turn this into a technique. Lowering the gear to reduce Vmc trades a control benefit for a large drag penalty and destroys whatever single-engine climb you had. It's a good exam answer about aerodynamics, and a bad plan in the airplane.
