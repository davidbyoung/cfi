### Question

What's the difference between propeller synchronization and synchrophasing?

### Answer

- **Synchronization** matches the **RPM** of both propellers, eliminating the slow "beat" you hear when they are slightly different.
- **Synchrophasing** goes further and controls the **relative blade position** between the two propellers, so blades don't pass the fuselage at the same instant. That cuts cabin noise and vibration beyond what matching RPM alone achieves.

Both are comfort systems, not safety systems.

The operational point: **turn them off before an engine failure situation and for takeoff and landing**, per the AFM. A synchronizer chasing a failing engine can drive the good engine's RPM in an unhelpful direction, and it masks the RPM split that would otherwise be a useful cue.
