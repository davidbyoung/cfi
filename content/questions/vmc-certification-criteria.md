### Question

Under what conditions is the published V<sub>MC</sub> determined?

### Answer

Legacy [§ 23.149](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2014-title14-vol1/pdf/CFR-2014-title14-vol1-sec23-149.pdf) set the conditions, deliberately the most unfavorable realistic combination. What the rule itself says:

- **Bank angle of not more than 5°** toward the operating engine, holding straight flight at the same speed after the critical engine is suddenly made inoperative - (a).
- **V<sub>MC</sub> for takeoff may not exceed 1.2 V<sub>S1</sub>**, with V<sub>S1</sub> at maximum takeoff weight - (b).
- **Most unfavorable weight and center of gravity**, **airborne with ground effect negligible** - (b). For a light twin the most unfavorable CG is the **aft** limit, because it shortens the rudder arm.
- **Maximum available takeoff power initially on each engine** - (b)(1).
- **Trimmed for takeoff** - (b)(2).
- **Flaps in the takeoff position** - (b)(3).
- **Landing gear retracted** - (b)(4).
- **All propeller controls in the recommended takeoff position** throughout - (b)(5).
- **Rudder pedal force not exceeding 150 lb**, and **no need to reduce power** on the operating engine - (e).
- No dangerous attitude, and it must be possible to prevent a **heading change of more than 20°** - (e).

Two conditions people recite that **aren't in the rule's text**. Both come from the ACS's configuration for the V<sub>MC</sub> demonstration Task:

- **Windmilling propeller** - indirect, but not wrong. (b)(5) keeps the propeller controls in the recommended takeoff position, and on a twin without autofeather that leaves the failed propeller windmilling. An airplane certified with autofeather is a different case - know which one you fly.
- **Cowl flaps set for takeoff** - not in the rule at all.

Paragraph (c) adds the landing configuration, but exempts reciprocating airplanes of 6,000 lb or less, which is every piston trainer.

Knowing this list is what lets you reason about every "what happens to V<sub>MC</sub> if..." question, because each item is a variable you can move.
