### Question

Under what conditions is the published Vmc determined?

### Answer

Legacy § 23.149 set the conditions, and they are deliberately the most unfavorable realistic combination:

- **Maximum available takeoff power** initially on each engine
- **Most unfavorable weight** and **most unfavorable center of gravity** (aft CG)
- Airplane **trimmed for takeoff**
- **Flaps in the takeoff position**
- **Landing gear retracted**
- Cowl flaps in the normal takeoff position
- Propeller of the inoperative engine **windmilling**, in the position it automatically assumes
- Airplane **airborne and out of ground effect**
- Bank angle of **not more than 5°** toward the operating engine
- Rudder force not exceeding **150 lb**
- Vmc for takeoff may not exceed **1.2 Vs1** at maximum takeoff weight

Knowing this list is what lets you reason about every "what happens to Vmc if..." question, because each item is a variable you can move.
