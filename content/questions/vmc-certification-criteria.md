### Question

Under what conditions is the published Vmc determined?

### Answer

Legacy **§ 23.149** set the conditions, and they are deliberately the most unfavorable realistic combination. What the rule itself says:

- **Bank angle of not more than 5°** toward the operating engine, maintaining straight flight at the same speed after the critical engine is suddenly made inoperative - § 23.149(a).
- **Vmc for takeoff may not exceed 1.2 Vs1**, with Vs1 determined at maximum takeoff weight - § 23.149(b).
- **Most unfavorable weight and center of gravity**, with the airplane **airborne and ground effect negligible** - § 23.149(b). For a light twin the most unfavorable CG is the **aft** limit, because it shortens the rudder arm.
- **Maximum available takeoff power initially on each engine** - § 23.149(b)(1).
- **Trimmed for takeoff** - (b)(2).
- **Flaps in the takeoff position** - (b)(3).
- **Landing gear retracted** - (b)(4).
- **All propeller controls in the recommended takeoff position** throughout - (b)(5).
- At Vmc, **rudder pedal force may not exceed 150 lb**, and **it must not be necessary to reduce power** on the operating engine - § 23.149(e).
- During the maneuver the airplane must not assume any dangerous attitude, and it must be possible to prevent a **heading change of more than 20°** - § 23.149(e).

**Two conditions people recite that are not in § 23.149.** The failed propeller **windmilling** (or feathered if an automatic feathering device is installed) and **cowl flaps in the normal takeoff position** come from the Airplane Flying Handbook's description, not from the rule's text. They are still the right operational picture - a windmilling propeller is what raises Vmc, which is the whole point of the feathered-vs-windmilling comparison - just don't attribute them to the CFR.

Note also that § 23.149(c) requires the landing configuration to be evaluated as well, but **only** for airplanes other than reciprocating-engine airplanes of 6,000 lb or less. Essentially every piston trainer - Seminole, Seneca, Duchess, Baron 58 - falls under that exception, so the takeoff configuration is the one that produced your red line.

Knowing this list is what lets you reason about every "what happens to Vmc if..." question, because each item is a variable you can move.
