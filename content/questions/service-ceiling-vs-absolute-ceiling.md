### Question

What's the difference between a service ceiling and an absolute ceiling?

### Answer

- **Service ceiling** - The density altitude at which the airplane can still climb at **50 fpm** in a clean configuration at best rate of climb speed.
- **Absolute ceiling** - The density altitude at which the airplane can no longer climb at all. Rate of climb is **zero**, and Vx and Vy have converged to a single speed.

Both are defined for the airplane with all engines operating. The single-engine versions use the same definitions with one engine inoperative and its propeller feathered.
