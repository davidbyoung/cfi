### Question

What lighting allows you to continue down to the runway?

### Answer

- Red terminating bar or red side row bars on ALSF-1 and ALSF-2 systems
- Runway threshold
- Threshold lights
- Runway end identifier lights (REILs)
- PAPI or VASI
- Runway lights
- Touchdown zone markings or lights
