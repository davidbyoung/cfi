### Question

What's the difference between an ODP and a SID?

### Answer

|                  | ODP                                                                                                       | SID                                                            |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Purpose          | Obstacle clearance only                                                                                   | Obstacle clearance, plus reduced pilot and controller workload |
| Format           | Textual or graphic. Graphic versions have "(OBSTACLE)" in the title                                       | Always graphic                                                 |
| Clearance        | Part 91 can fly one without a clearance, but tell ATC you're doing it                                     | You must be cleared for it                                     |
| Where to find it | Front of the TPP under "Takeoff Minimums and (Obstacle) Departure Procedures," or charted with the plates | Charted with the airport's approach plates                     |

- Either type may be conventional or RNAV. RNAV versions have "RNAV" in the title and require RNAV 1 performance.
- Part 91 isn't required to fly an ODP, but you should strongly consider it at night, in marginal VMC or IMC, or at an unfamiliar airport with terrain.
- If you don't want a SID, put "NO SIDs" in the remarks section of your flight plan.
