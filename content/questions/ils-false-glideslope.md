### Question

What is a false glideslope, and how do you avoid capturing one?

### Answer

- The glideslope antenna radiates additional lobes above the true glidepath. The first one that looks usable shows up at roughly twice the true angle, around 6° on a 3° glideslope, and sensing can be reversed on some of them.
- The defense is procedural: **intercept the glideslope from below** at the published glideslope intercept altitude, and never chase the needle down from above.
- Cross-check the charted crossing altitude at the FAF or marker. An intercept that begins unusually high, or a descent rate roughly double what you expected, is the giveaway.
