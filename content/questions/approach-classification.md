### Question

How are instrument approaches classified?

### Answer

- **Precision** - Lateral and vertical guidance meeting precision approach standards, flown to a **DA**: ILS, PAR, GLS.
- **APV (approach with vertical guidance)** - Vertical guidance that doesn't meet precision standards, also flown to a **DA**: LPV, LNAV/VNAV, and LDA with glidepath.
- **Non-precision** - Lateral guidance only, flown to an **MDA**: LOC, LOC BC, LDA, SDF, VOR, NDB, LP, LNAV, and ASR.
