### Question

When do you need to slow down for a hold?

### Answer

- Begin slowing within **3 minutes** of the holding fix so that you cross it at or below the maximum holding speed.
- The standard maximums are:
  - Up to 6,000 ft MSL - 200 kt
  - 6,001 to 14,000 ft MSL - 230 kt
  - 14,001 ft MSL and above - 265 kt
- A speed in parentheses inside the charted holding pattern, e.g., (175), replaces the standard maximum. A pattern restricted to 210 kt between 6,001 and 14,000 ft is shown with an icon instead.
- If you can't comply with a published speed restriction, tell ATC.
