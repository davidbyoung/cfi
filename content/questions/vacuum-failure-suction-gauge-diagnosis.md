### Question

You’re flying a steam gauge airplane, and start getting confused by the conflicting information your instruments are displaying. You look at your suction gauge and notice it’s low. What’s wrong?

### Answer

You have a vacuum system failure, and are likely to lose your attitude indicator and directional gyro
