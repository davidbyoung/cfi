### Question

What's different about fuel management in a twin?

### Answer

- Twins usually have **more tanks** - mains, auxiliaries, and sometimes tip tanks - each with its own rules about when it may be used.
- Many AFMs restrict auxiliary tanks to **level cruise only**, and require takeoff and landing on the **mains**.
- Some aux systems **return** unused fuel to a main tank, so running a main tank full and then selecting aux can vent fuel overboard.
- Unequal burn creates **lateral imbalance**, which matters more in a twin because you may already be holding a rolling moment.
- Each engine normally feeds from its own side, so "total fuel on board" overstates what a single operating engine can actually reach without crossfeed.

The habit worth building: know which tank feeds which engine, and track the burn on each side rather than in aggregate.
