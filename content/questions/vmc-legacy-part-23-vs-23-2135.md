### Question

The Vmc criteria you memorized come from § 23.149, but that section isn't in the current regulations. What happened?

### Answer

- The 2017 rewrite of part 23 replaced the old prescriptive airworthiness standards with performance-based ones. **§ 23.149 no longer exists** in the current CFR.
- Its replacement, **§ 23.2135**, requires the airplane to be controllable and maneuverable in the critical configurations without specifying the 5° bank, the 150 lb rudder force, or the 1.2 Vs1 cap.
- Essentially every light twin you will train or fly in was certified under the **legacy** rule, so the old criteria are still the ones that describe your airplane.

The practical answer for an examiner: quote the § 23.149 conditions, and know that they come from the version of part 23 your airplane was certified under rather than from today's CFR.
