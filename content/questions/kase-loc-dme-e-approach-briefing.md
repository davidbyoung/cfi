### Question

Brief the [KASE LOC/DME-E approach](https://www.airnav.com/depart?http://aeronav.faa.gov/d-tpp/2608/05889LDE.PDF)

### Answer

Interesting notes:

- NA when temperature is -21°C or below
- On missed, climbing right turn to track I-PKN backcourse outbound until LINDZ, followed by R-244 to GLENO and hold
- VGSI and descent angles not coincident
