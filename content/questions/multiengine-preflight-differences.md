### Question

What do you look at on a twin's preflight that you wouldn't on a single?

### Answer

- **Both engines**, obviously - but specifically oil levels on each, and the cowl flaps on each.
- **Propellers** - Blade condition, and check for **oil or grease at the hub**, which can indicate a seal leak that would eventually prevent feathering.
- **Fuel** - More tanks, more sumps, more drains. Sump every one, and confirm the quantity in each rather than a total.
- **Nacelles and gear** - Hydraulic leaks, brake lines, gear doors and linkages, squat switch.
- **Crossfeed and fuel selector** - Check for freedom of movement and correct detents.
- **Deice and anti-ice** - Boot condition, prop boots, alcohol quantity, heated stall warning and pitot.
- **Nose baggage** - Loaded and secured within limits, because it affects CG meaningfully.

The mindset difference: you are inspecting two of most things, and a discrepancy on the side you overlook is the one that quits.
