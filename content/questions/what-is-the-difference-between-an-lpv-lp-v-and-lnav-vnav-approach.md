### Question

What is the difference between an LPV, LP+V, and LNAV/VNAV approach?

### Answer

Localizer Performance with Vertical guidance (LPV) - Precision-like approach with lateral and vertical guidance that becomes more sensitive closer to the runway

- Requires WAAS
- Think of it as GPS guidance that behaves similarly to an ILS

Localizer Performance with advisory Vertical guidance (LP+V) – Non-precision approach with localizer-performance lateral navigation and advisory vertical guidance

- May have obstacles or step-down fixes along the approach path
- Think of it as GPS lateral guidance similar to a LOC approach, with advisory vertical guidance
- It will not be listed as a separate line of minima on the IAP - it appears on the GPS unit itself

Lateral Navigation/Vertical Navigation (LNAV/VNAV) - Precision-like approach with lateral and vertical guidance, but without increasing angular guidance like LPV

- Requires WAAS or barometric VNAV
- Frequently has temperature restrictions if using barometric VNAV
- WAAS-equipped aircraft will almost always default to LPV over LNAV/VNAV
