### Question

What is fuel crossfeed for, and what are its limitations?

### Answer

**It lets an operating engine draw fuel from the tank on the opposite side.**

- Purpose is to use the fuel on the failed engine's side and to keep the airplane **laterally balanced** on a long single-engine leg.
- It is **not** a way to feed both engines from one tank in normal operations.
- Typical limitations: use in **level cruise only**, and **prohibited for takeoff and landing**.
- Procedures are highly airplane-specific - which selector, which boost pump, and in what order. Getting it wrong can starve the running engine.

Treat it as a checklist item, never a memory item. It also isn't urgent: get the airplane cleaned up, stabilized, and headed somewhere before reconfiguring fuel.
