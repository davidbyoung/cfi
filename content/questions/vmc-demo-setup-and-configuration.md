### Question

How do you configure the airplane for a V<sub>MC</sub> demonstration?

### Answer

Set up to approximate the certification conditions, which is what makes the demonstration meaningful:

- **Landing gear up**
- **Flaps in the takeoff position**
- **Cowl flaps** as required
- Trimmed for takeoff
- Both engines set to a safe starting power, then the **critical engine** reduced to idle (or simulated) and **maximum power** on the operating engine
- Propeller of the simulated failed engine **windmilling** (not feathered)
- Bank **up to 5° toward the operating engine**

Do it at an altitude that allows recovery **no lower than 3,000 ft AGL**, and clear the area thoroughly first.
