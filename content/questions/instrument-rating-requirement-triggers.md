### Question

When do you need an instrument rating?

### Answer

To act as PIC for a flight:

- Under IFR
- Weather that does not meet VFR minimums
- Anytime in Class A airspace
- Special VFR at night
- Carrying passengers for hire at night or on a flight more than 50 NM
