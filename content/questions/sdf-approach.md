### Question

What is an SDF approach, and how does it differ from a localizer or an LDA?

### Answer

- Simplified Directional Facility. It transmits on localizer frequencies (108.10 to 111.95 MHz) and you fly it much like a localizer, but with less precision.
- The course is typically **6° or 12° wide**, instead of a localizer's 3° to 6° width tailored to the runway, so full scale deflection covers a lot more ground.
- The course **may or may not be aligned with the runway**. A localizer is always aligned with the runway, and an LDA specifically is not.
- There's no glideslope, so it's a non-precision approach flown to an MDA.
- Usable off-course indications are limited to 35° either side of the course centerline. Disregard anything outside of that.
