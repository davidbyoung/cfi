### Question

What are the different types of VOR checks?

### Answer

Ground:

- VOR Test Facility (VOT): +/- 4°
  - Set the course to 360°, and it should be centered with a FROM indication
  - Set the course to 180°, and it should be centered with a TO indication
- Certified ground checkpoint: +/- 4°
  - Found in Chart Supplement
  - Follow instructions on local signage

Air:

- Airborne checkpoint: +/- 6°
  - Select a VOR radial that lies along the centerline of a VOR airway
  - Select a prominent ground point roughly 20 NM from the VOR that is along that radial
  - Note the indicated VOR bearing as compared to the charted course
- Dual check: +/- 4°
  - Tune both nav radios to same station and center both
