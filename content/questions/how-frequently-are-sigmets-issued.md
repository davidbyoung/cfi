### Question

How frequently are SIGMETs issued?

### Answer

- As needed, valid for 4 hr, except SIGMETs associated with tropical cyclones and volcanic ash clouds, which are valid for 6 hr
- Severe icing, severe turbulence, volcanic ash, tropical cyclones/hurricanes, and duststorms/sandstorms (all not associated with thunderstorms)
