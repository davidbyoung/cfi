### Question

What altitude and airspeed limits apply to simulated engine failures on an MEI practical test?

### Answer

- **Engine failure after liftoff:** not simulated until at least **400 ft AGL** and at least V<sub>SSE</sub>, V<sub>XSE</sub>, or V<sub>YSE</sub>.
- **Single-engine demonstration tasks:** the evaluator picks an entry altitude that lets them finish **no lower than 3,000 ft AGL**, or the manufacturer's recommended altitude, whichever is higher.
- **Anything requiring feathering or shutdown:** only at a position and altitude from which a **safe landing at an established airport** is possible if the propeller will not unfeather or the engine will not restart.
- If it will not unfeather or restart in flight, **treat it as an emergency**, not as a training exercise that went long.

The airplane you supply also has to permit feathering in flight. An airplane that prohibits it cannot be used for the test.
