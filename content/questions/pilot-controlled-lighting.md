### Question

How do you know if an airport has pilot-controlled lighting? How do you use it? When should you use it?

### Answer

Pilot-controlled lighting is indicated with an "(L)" symbol on VFR sectional and IFR enroute charts. To use it, tune to the UNICOM frequency and key the mic 3 times for low-intensity lighting, 5 times for medium-intensity lighting, and 7 times for high-intensity lighting. The lighting will remain on for 15 minutes. You should use it when it's dark out prior to approach to landing. While on an instrument approach, you should turn it on during your final approach segment so you can see the runway at nighttime after breaking out of IMC.
