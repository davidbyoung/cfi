### Question

Let’s say you’re proceeding direct to RUDGE, and are told “Cross RUDGE at 6,000 ft, you are cleared for the RNAV 7 approach”. What do you do when you get to RUDGE?

### Answer

- Enter a 4 NM hold, and start your descent to 2,500 ft
- Upon reaching 2,500 ft and you’re on the inbound leg, you may continue to CUDUP
