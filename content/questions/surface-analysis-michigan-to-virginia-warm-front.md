### Question

Refer to the surface analysis chart from the source guide.

![Surface analysis chart](../assets/surface-analysis-chart.png)

What is the red line stretching from western Michigan to Virginia, and what kind of conditions are associated with it?

### Answer

- A warm front, where a warm air mass advances over a cooler air mass
- Weather changes are slow, and may include light rain, drizzle, and a temperature rise
