### Question

After you have the airplane under control, do you try to restart the engine or secure it?

### Answer

It depends on altitude, workload, and whether you have any idea what happened.

- **Secure it** if you are low, busy, close to an airport, or the cause is obvious and serious - fire, severe vibration, oil loss, mechanical noise. Never restart an engine that may be damaged or burning.
- **Consider a restart** only with **plenty of altitude**, the airplane stabilized and cleaned up, and a plausible cause you can address: fuel selector on an empty tank, a switched-off boost pump, carburetor ice, a magneto problem.

Order of operations matters. Fly the airplane, clean up the drag, get to a stable single-engine condition and on the way to an airport first. Troubleshoot only with whatever attention is genuinely left over, and always with the checklist.
