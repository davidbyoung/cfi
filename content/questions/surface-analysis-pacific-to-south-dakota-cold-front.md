### Question

Refer to the surface analysis chart from the source guide.

![Surface analysis chart](../assets/surface-analysis-chart.png)

What is the blue line stretching from the Pacific up to South Dakota, and what kind of conditions are associated with it?

### Answer

- A cold front, where a cold air mass advances under and lifts a warmer air mass
- Weather changes are abrupt and typically more severe, including thunderstorms, heavy rainfall, and a temperature drop
