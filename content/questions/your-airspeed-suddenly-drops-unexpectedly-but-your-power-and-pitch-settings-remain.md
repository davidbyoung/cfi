### Question

Your airspeed suddenly drops unexpectedly, but your power and pitch settings remain stable. How do you troubleshoot?

### Answer

Suspect a blocked pitot/ram air source. Turn pitot heat on and see if the issue resolves. Use known pitch and power settings to land safely if not.
