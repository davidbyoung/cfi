### Question

Refer to the surface analysis chart from the source guide.

![Surface analysis chart](../assets/surface-analysis-chart.png)

What is the red “L” over Utah, and what kind of conditions are associated with it?

### Answer

- A low-pressure system, with air generally rising, moving inward, and rotating counterclockwise
- It results in unstable conditions such as rain, wind, icing, and even thunderstorms
