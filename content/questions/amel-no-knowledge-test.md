### Question

Why isn't there a written knowledge test for a multiengine add-on, and how should that change the way you prepare?

### Answer

- [§ 61.63(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-61/subpart-B/section-61.63) requires only training, an endorsement, and a practical test for an additional class rating, so there's nothing to screen your knowledge before you meet the examiner.
- That pushes **all** of the knowledge evaluation into the oral. Expect it to be longer and deeper than the add-on's short flight portion suggests.
- The weight lands on the topics unique to twins: Vmc and everything that moves it, the critical engine, zero sideslip, one-engine-inoperative (OEI) performance, and systems.
