### Question

What is MVFR?

### Answer

Marginal VFR. It's weather that's approaching IFR, but is still legally VFR - **1,000-3,000 ft** ceilings and/or **3-5 SM** visibility. If you are instrument rated, it's a good idea to file IFR in these conditions.
