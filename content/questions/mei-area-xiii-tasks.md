### Question

Which multiengine Tasks does the evaluator have to test on an MEI practical test?

### Answer

The ACS's **Multiengine Operations** area has three Tasks and the note is unambiguous: the evaluator must assess **all** of them.

- **Maneuvering with One Engine Inoperative**
- **V<sub>MC</sub> Demonstration**
- **Demonstration of Effects of Various Airspeeds and Configurations during Engine Inoperative Performance**

Beyond that area:

- In **Technical Subject Areas**, the evaluator must select **One Engine Inoperative Performance** for a multiengine applicant, on top of the Tasks required of everyone.
- In **Emergency Operations**, there are multiengine-only Tasks for engine failure during takeoff before V<sub>MC</sub> and engine failure after liftoff.
- At least one landing must be demonstrated and explained with a **simulated feathered propeller** at zero thrust.

There is no picking a favorite. The multiengine block is tested in full.
