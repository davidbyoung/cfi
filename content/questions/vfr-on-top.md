### Question

What is VFR-on-top? When would you use it? How do you request it? Are there any rules you must follow while VFR-on-top?

### Answer

**What it is**

VFR-on-top is an ATC authorization that allows an aircraft operating on an IFR flight plan to fly in VFR conditions at an appropriate VFR altitude instead of an ATC-assigned altitude. The flight is still operating on an IFR flight plan; VFR-on-top does not cancel IFR.

**When you'd use it**

You might request VFR-on-top when you want the flexibility to choose your own altitude while remaining on an IFR flight plan, e.g., to fly above or between cloud layers, avoid turbulence, or obtain better weather conditions. You may also request a climb to VFR-on-top to climb through a cloud layer into VMC.

**How to request it**

To request it, you can call ATC and say "Chicago Center, Archer 3096B, request VFR-on-top".

**Rules to follow**

While VFR-on-top, you must maintain VFR weather minimums and appropriate VFR cruising altitudes, remain above IFR minimum altitudes, continue complying with your IFR clearance and applicable IFR procedures, and see and avoid other traffic. It is not permitted in Class A airspace.
