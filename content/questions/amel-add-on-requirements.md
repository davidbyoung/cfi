### Question

What do you need to add an airplane multiengine land (AMEL) class rating to a certificate you already hold?

### Answer

- **No knowledge (written) test.** § 61.63(c) requires only training, an endorsement, and a practical test for an additional class rating.
- A logbook endorsement from an authorized instructor certifying you received training and are proficient in the areas of operation that apply.
- A practical test at the level of the certificate you already hold - a private pilot tests to the Private ACS, a commercial pilot to the Commercial ACS.
- **No minimum flight hour requirement** for the class add-on itself. Proficiency is the standard, not a number.
