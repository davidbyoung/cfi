### Question

What is DME/DME? What about DME/DME/IRU?

### Answer

DME/DME is a form of RNAV that uses two DME stations to ascertain your location. It is commonly combined with an inertial reference unit (IRU) to form DME/DME/IRU. It is an alternative to GPS for RNAV.
