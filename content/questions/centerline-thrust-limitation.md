### Question

What is the "Limited to Center Thrust" limitation, how do you end up with one, and how do you remove it?

### Answer

- You receive it when you complete the AMEL practical test in a multiengine airplane that **does not have a manufacturer's published V<sub>MC</sub>** - for example a Cessna 337, whose engines are in line with the fuselage and produce no asymmetric thrust.
- The published V<sub>MC</sub> appears on the **type certificate data sheet (TCDS)** or in the AFM. That's the actual test, not simply where the engines are mounted.
- It restricts you to multiengine airplanes that don't produce asymmetric thrust.
- To remove it, take a practical test on the multiengine Tasks in a multiengine airplane that **does** have a published V<sub>MC</sub>.
