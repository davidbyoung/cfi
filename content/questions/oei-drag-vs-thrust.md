### Question

Why does a small amount of extra drag hurt so much with one engine inoperative?

### Answer

- In normal two-engine flight you have a large surplus of thrust over drag, and that surplus is what climbs the airplane. Adding drag just eats into a healthy margin.
- With one engine out, the surplus is nearly gone. Every additional unit of drag comes straight out of climb rate.
- A windmilling propeller, extended gear, extended flaps, or flying with a sideslip can each individually erase what little climb you have.

This is why the OEI flow is fundamentally about **drag reduction**, and why the cleanup happens before you try to troubleshoot anything.
