### Question

What are the types of hypoxia, and what causes each?

### Answer

- **Hypoxic** - Not enough oxygen pressure available for oxygen to cross into the bloodstream. This is the altitude type.
- **Hypemic** - The blood can't carry the oxygen. Carbon monoxide poisoning, anemia, blood loss, or a recent blood donation.
- **Stagnant** - The blood isn't moving well. Heart problems, G loading, or cold restricting circulation to the extremities.
- **Histotoxic** - The cells can't use the oxygen they're given. Alcohol or drugs.

Symptoms to catch early include euphoria or belligerence, degraded judgment, headache, tunnel vision, and cyanosis in the fingernails and lips. Night vision starts degrading as low as 5,000 ft, and performance can seriously deteriorate within 15 minutes at 15,000 ft. The fix is oxygen and a descent. Since the symptoms overlap with hyperventilation, treat for hypoxia first when you're not sure.
