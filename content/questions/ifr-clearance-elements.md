### Question

What are the elements of an IFR clearance, and what does "cleared as filed" actually clear you for?

### Answer

**CRAFT**

- **C** - Clearance limit
- **R** - Route
- **A** - Altitude (initial altitude, plus what to expect and when)
- **F** - Frequency for departure
- **T** - Transponder code

**Abbreviated clearances**

- An abbreviated clearance, e.g., "cleared to KMSN as filed," still names the destination and includes any departure procedure, the altitude information, the departure frequency, and the squawk. Only the route is abbreviated.
- It's only issued when you actually have a flight plan on file, and it clears you via the route you filed. Any change ATC made will be read to you in full.
- A clearance isn't authority to deviate from a regulation, and accepting one doesn't transfer terrain avoidance to ATC below the minimum IFR altitude. If you can't comply or aren't sure what changed, say so.
