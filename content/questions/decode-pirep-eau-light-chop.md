### Question

Decode this PIREP: EAU UA /OV EAU/TM 2247/FL340/TP CRJ7/TB OCNL LGT CHOP

### Answer

Routine PIREP over EAU, time 2247Z, flight level 340, aircraft type CRJ-700, occasional turbulence and light chop
