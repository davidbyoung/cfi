### Question

What are the different types of fog, and how do they form?

### Answer

| Type                  | How It Forms                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Radiation Fog**     | The ground cools at night and cools the air directly above it to its dew point. Most likely with **clear skies, light winds, and high humidity**. |
| **Advection Fog**     | **Moist air moves over a colder surface** and is cooled to its dew point. Common in coastal areas.                                                |
| **Steam Fog**         | **Cold, dry air moves over warmer water**. Water evaporates into the cold air and quickly condenses, producing fog just above the surface.        |
| **Upslope Fog**       | **Moist, stable air is forced uphill**, where it expands and cools to its dew point.                                                              |
| **Precipitation Fog** | **Warm rain falls through cooler air** below, adding moisture until the cooler air becomes saturated.                                             |
| **Freezing Fog**      | Fog consists of **supercooled liquid water droplets** that remain liquid below freezing and freeze when they contact a surface.                   |
