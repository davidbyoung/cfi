### Question

You have an engine fire in flight. What do you do?

### Answer

Follow the AFM, but the general shape is:

- **Shut down and secure the burning engine** - Mixture idle cutoff, fuel selector **off**, fuel pump off, and **feather** the propeller. Removing the fuel supply is the priority.
- Turn off that side's electrical items as directed - alternator field, and other nonessential loads.
- **Do not attempt a restart** on an engine that has been on fire.
- Fly the airplane on one engine using the normal OEI flow.
- **Land as soon as possible** - Not "as soon as practical." An extinguished fire can reignite, and structure may be compromised.
- Declare an emergency and tell ATC what you need.

Some AFMs suggest increasing airspeed to help extinguish the fire; know whether yours does rather than improvising.
