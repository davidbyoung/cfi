### Question

How far out is a VOR signal reliable?

### Answer

Legacy:

- Terminal (T):
  - 1,000-12,000 ft - 25 NM
- Low (L):
  - 1,000-18,000 ft - 40 NM
- High (H):
  - 1,000-14,500 ft - 40 NM
  - 14,500-18,000 ft - 100 NM
  - 18,000-45,000 ft - 130 NM
  - 45,000-60,000 ft - 100 NM

Minimum Operational Network (MON):

- Low (VL):
  - 1,000-5,000 ft - 40 NM
  - 5,000-18,000 ft - 70 NM
- High (VH):
  - 1,000-5,000 ft - 40 NM
  - 5,000-14,500 ft - 70 NM
  - 14,500-18,000 ft - 100 NM
  - 18,000-45,000 ft - 130 NM
  - 45,000-60,000 ft - 100 NM
