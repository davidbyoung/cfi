### Question

Why might a two-person training flight need ballast in the back?

### Answer

Because with only an instructor and a student on board and no bags, some twins sit **ahead of the forward CG limit**.

- The fix is ballast in the aft baggage compartment, secured and accounted for on the weight and balance.
- It is the opposite of the loading problem most pilots are trained to watch for, which is why it gets missed.

It also cuts against you in a way worth teaching. A forward CG gives better stall and spin avoidance characteristics, which is welcome on a flight full of engine failures and V<sub>MC</sub> work, but it also **lengthens the rudder's moment arm**, so it is not a free lunch: the airplane you are demonstrating V<sub>MC</sub> in behaves slightly differently from the one loaded to the aft limit that certification assumed.
